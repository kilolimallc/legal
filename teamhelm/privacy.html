Teamhelm

Privacy Policy

Teamhelm is a shared schedule for a team, so unlike our single-user apps it necessarily keeps some data on a server — the schedule everyone reads has to live somewhere they can all reach it. This policy describes exactly what that data is, who can see it, and what never happens to it.

Effective: September 1, 2026

Your Account

You sign in with Apple, or with a one-time code sent to your email address. Teamhelm stores your account's email address and the display name you choose. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, Teamhelm only ever sees the forwarding address Apple creates. Your name is shown only to people on your own teams.

Team Data

Rosters, schedules, time-off requests, shift swaps, bids, training records, and team messages are stored server-side so that everyone on a team reads one shared copy. Access follows the team: this data is visible only to members of the team it belongs to, and some of it — time-off requests and bids — only to the person who made them and whoever runs the team. Kilo Lima Management LLC staff do not browse team data; it is accessed only to resolve a support request you initiate, or as required by law.

Contact Details on the Roster

Whoever runs a team may add phone numbers and email addresses for people on the roster, so colleagues can reach each other. These are visible to the whole team, and the app says so at the point of entry. If you import people from your device's Contacts, the reading happens on your device: only the people you pick, and only the details you confirm, are uploaded. The app never uploads your address book.

Photos

A team may wear a photo, and each person may add one to their roster entry — from the photo library or the camera; a photo taken in the app is used for that and nothing else. Photos are shown to the team. They are served from web links that are not listed or searchable anywhere, but anyone who has the exact link can view the image, so don't use a photo you wouldn't show outside the team. Replacing or removing a photo takes effect immediately.

The Calendar Feed

A manager may turn on a read-only calendar link so released schedules appear in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook. Anyone who holds that link can read the released schedule, which is why the app treats the link itself as the key: turning the feed off, or minting a new link, cuts off every copy of the old one.

Email the App Sends

Teamhelm sends email in four cases: sign-in codes you request; invitations a team lead reads and approves before they go out; copies of in-app notifications, which are off by default and sent only if you turn them on, only to your own account's address; and messages a lead composes in the app — a released schedule sent to company addresses the team has set, or a hand-over notice to addresses the owner has set. In every composed case the lead sees and can edit exactly what goes out before it goes. Delivery goes through our email provider, Brevo, which processes recipient addresses solely to deliver the message. Every notification email tells you how to turn them off.

Notifications

If you allow push notifications, Apple issues a device token that Teamhelm stores in order to deliver them. Each kind of notification can be switched off per team, and tokens for devices that no longer exist are discarded.

What We Do Not Do

Teamhelm contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no usage telemetry. Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers — not any of it, not ever. The optional in-app support report includes account and team identifiers so we can find the same records, but never your email address, your name, or anything you have written in a request.

Where Data Lives

Team data is stored in a managed database hosted by Supabase in the United States, protected by row-level access rules so that one team's data is not reachable from another's.

Deletion

A team that has never published a schedule can be deleted outright by its owner, and everything in it goes. Once a team has published schedules, what people were told to work is retained so the team's own records stay readable; people removed from a roster stop being scheduled but remain named on days they actually worked. To delete your account and the data attached to it, email kilolimallc@icloud.com from the account's address and we will handle it promptly.

Children's Privacy

Teamhelm is a workplace scheduling tool and is not directed at children under 13. Kilo Lima Management LLC does not knowingly collect information from children.
