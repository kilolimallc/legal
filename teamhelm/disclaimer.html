Teamhelm

Disclaimer

Teamhelm keeps track of who is scheduled, who is away, and when training is due. It is a planning tool for the person running a team — and only that.

Effective: August 26, 2026

Training and Currency

Your operator's records are the ones that count. Where the app shows a check as current, that is a calculation from the dates somebody typed in — not a certified record, and not a substitute for the paperwork your operation is required to keep.

Nobody should fly on a date this app displays. Confirm currency against your own records before anyone is rostered or dispatched. The app can only be as right as what it was told: a check entered against the wrong month, or not entered at all, produces a confident answer that is wrong.

The Checks It Sets Up for You

The checks a Part 135 team starts with are a starting point, not a rulebook. Every one of them can be edited, and your operations specifications, training program, and any authority you answer to take precedence over anything set up here. Rules differ by country, by operator, and over time; this app does not track those changes and does not tell you what your obligations are.

What It Doesn't Do

Teamhelm does not calculate flight time, duty time, or rest. It schedules days of coverage. If you need duty and rest limits, that is a different job and this app does not do it.
