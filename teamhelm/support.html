Before You Write

If you're reporting something the schedule did that you didn't expect, it helps a lot if you can include:

• Which team, and which schedule — its name and the date the issue applies to.
• What you did, what the app showed, and what you expected it to show.
• Whether the schedule had been released, or was still a draft.

Screenshots of the affected screens are usually faster than describing the layout in text.
